Project Title:
NextTalent - AI-Powered Future Talent Ecosystem for Employment and Skills Development

Case Alignment:
This project is developed based on URIIS Case Study 3:
"Future Talent Ecosystem: AI for Employment and Skills"

Executive Summary:
NextTalent is an AI-driven web platform that helps university students bridge the gap between education and employment. Using voice input and resume intelligence, it generates personalized career roadmaps, identifies skill gaps, recommends practical learning resources, and provides aggregate workforce insights for institutions and policymakers. The platform supports career readiness at the individual level while contributing data-informed planning at the ecosystem level.

------------------------------
1) THE PROBLEM
------------------------------

1.1 Core Problem Statement
Malaysian students often face uncertainty when transitioning from university to employment due to:
- Limited clarity on career pathways and skill requirements.
- Mismatch between current competencies and industry demand.
- Fragmented access to trusted, affordable upskilling resources.
- Low confidence in personal career planning.

1.2 Why This Problem Matters
- Graduates may remain underprepared for high-demand roles.
- Employers struggle to find candidates with job-ready skill sets.
- Universities need stronger signals to improve curriculum relevance.
- National talent planning requires better real-time skills intelligence.

1.3 Current Pain Points
- Career guidance is often generic, not personalized.
- Students do not know "what to learn next" after identifying weaknesses.
- Skill audits are manual and inconsistent.
- Decision-makers lack clear dashboards on emerging talent gaps.

------------------------------
2) TARGET USER
------------------------------

2.1 Primary Users
- University students (all disciplines): career exploration, skill benchmarking, learning direction.
- Final-year students and fresh graduates: job readiness acceleration.

2.2 Secondary Users
- University career centers: trend monitoring, targeted intervention.
- Lecturers and program coordinators: identify common competency gaps.
- Employers and ecosystem partners: understand emerging talent profiles.
- Policymakers: workforce planning and upskilling priorities.

2.3 User Challenges
- "I know my interest, but not the exact skills needed."
- "I have a resume, but I do not know if it matches market expectations."
- "I do not have time to search many platforms for quality learning resources."
- "Institutions cannot easily aggregate and act on student talent data."

------------------------------
3) YOUR SOLUTION
------------------------------

3.1 Solution Overview
NextTalent is a 4-module digital platform:
1. Voice-to-Roadmap AI Assistant
2. Skill-Gap Analyzer
3. Micro-Learning Recommender
4. Workforce Analytics Dashboard

3.2 How It Works (End-to-End User Flow)
Step 1: Student speaks a target career or interest (voice input).
Step 2: AI generates a personalized career roadmap with milestones and required skills.
Step 3: Student uploads/pastes resume profile for skill-gap analysis.
Step 4: System calculates match level and highlights missing skills.
Step 5: Platform recommends relevant free/low-cost micro-learning courses.
Step 6: User tracks progress while institutions view anonymized trend analytics.

3.3 Technical Foundation
- Frontend: Next.js 15 + TypeScript
- AI engine: Google Gemini (roadmap generation and skill intelligence)
- Data storage: Supabase (with local fallback)
- Visualization: Chart.js dashboard
- Input modality: Browser-native Web Speech API

3.4 Functional Coverage
- Personalized career planning in under a minute.
- Evidence-based skill matching and gap detection.
- Actionable learning recommendations from curated course database.
- System-wide insights on trending careers and skill deficits.

------------------------------
4) INNOVATION ELEMENT
------------------------------

4.1 What Makes NextTalent Unique
- Voice-first career planning for speed and accessibility.
- Integrated "discover-analyze-learn" pipeline in one platform.
- AI-powered interpretation of unstructured student intent and resumes.
- Dual value model: individual career coaching + institutional analytics.

4.2 Differentiation from Existing Approaches
Most tools only do one function (job search, resume scan, or course listing).
NextTalent unifies:
- Career pathway generation
- Skill-gap diagnostics
- Learning recommendations
- Ecosystem-level trend intelligence

4.3 Practical Innovation
- Reduces friction for first-time users through conversational entry.
- Converts abstract career goals into concrete, sequenced action plans.
- Enables data-driven interventions by universities and policymakers.

------------------------------

5.1 Student-Level Impact
- Higher career clarity and confidence.
- Faster identification of employability gaps.
- Better learning focus and resource efficiency.
- Stronger resume-job alignment over time.

5.2 Institutional Impact
- Better understanding of common student weaknesses.
- Improved curriculum enhancement planning.
- More targeted career support programs.

5.3 National / Ecosystem Impact
- Supports workforce readiness and talent competitiveness.
- Promotes lifelong learning culture.
- Enables evidence-informed upskilling policies.

5.4 Suggested Measurable KPIs
- % increase in student career clarity score.

- Number of missing skills closed per user cohort.

- Career center intervention conversion rate.

------------------------------
6) FEASIBILITY
------------------------------

6.1 Implementation Feasibility
The current prototype already demonstrates core flow and technical viability.

6.2 Required Resources
- Product team: 1 project lead, 1-2 developers, 1 UX designer, 1 data/AI support.
- Institutional collaboration: universities and career offices.
- Content partnerships: learning providers, industry mentors.
- Infrastructure: cloud hosting, API usage budget, database operations.

6.3 Stakeholders
- Students and universities (primary adoption ecosystem)
- Government agencies related to higher education and talent
- Industry and employers for market alignment
- Platform sponsors and education partners

6.4 Deployment Approach (Phased)
Phase 1 (Pilot): 1-3 universities, selected faculties, usability and outcomes testing.
Phase 2 (Scale): Multi-campus rollout and expanded course catalog.
Phase 3 (National): University-wide ecosystem integration and policy analytics layer.

6.5 Key Risks and Mitigation
- AI output variability: use guardrails, prompt templates, and review loops.
- Data privacy concerns: consent flow, minimum data retention, anonymized analytics.
- User adoption barriers: onboarding walkthroughs and campus ambassador programs.
- Content quality drift: periodic curation and partner validation.
